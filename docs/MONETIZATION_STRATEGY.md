# Schulferienklar Monetization Strategy

Updated: 2026-07-27

## 1. Product position

Schulferienklar remains a free, trustworthy and low-distraction planning
service for German school holidays and public holidays.

It is not primarily an advertising website and it is not intended to replace
school administration platforms.

The long-term product position is:

> A reliable German school-holiday and public-holiday planning service for
> people, websites, applications and organisations.

## 2. Monetization principle

The core public service remains free.

Revenue should come primarily from:

1. professional widgets
2. verified and calculated data APIs
3. organisation-specific calendar and data feeds
4. white-label products
5. support and commercial-use guarantees

Display advertising is secondary and must never damage readability, trust or
the usefulness of the site.

## 3. Free public product

The following remain part of the free public product:

- school-holiday and public-holiday dates
- Bundesland and year calendars
- Urlaubsplaner
- Brückentage calculations
- multi-state comparisons
- Jahreskalender
- basic ICS, PDF and PNG downloads
- basic subscription calendars
- basic public JSON data
- official sources and review dates

## 4. What Schulferienklar will not do now

The following are not current priorities:

- a general student homework planner
- school messaging or administration
- accounts and cloud synchronisation
- a large consumer subscription product
- intrusive display advertising
- multiple widget products before the first widget is validated
- a paid raw-date API that duplicates existing free APIs
- large numbers of thin SEO pages

Student or family planning products may be considered later when user behaviour
shows clear recurring demand.

## 5. First distribution product

The first external product is:

> Nächste Schulferien Widget

The widget should show:

- selected Bundesland
- the next school holiday
- official start and end dates
- countdown until the holiday
- connected free period where available
- two or three following holiday periods
- official source
- last review date
- a small Schulferienklar attribution link

Technical principles:

- iframe based
- responsive
- no advertising
- no cookies
- no personal profiles
- no behavioural tracking
- automatic data updates
- stable embed URL
- safe fallback when the widget cannot load

## 6. Purpose of the free widget

The free widget is not initially a direct revenue product.

It is used to validate:

- whether external websites will install Schulferienklar
- which industries need the data
- whether partners request custom colours or layouts
- whether partners want multiple Bundesländer
- whether attribution removal has value
- whether API or custom-feed demand exists

Potential users include:

- tutoring and education providers
- holiday camps
- sports clubs
- family and regional portals
- hotels and campsites
- travel websites
- HR and employee portals
- associations and local organisations

## 7. Possible paid products

Paid products are built only after demand is observed.

### Widget Pro

Possible features:

- custom colours and typography
- multiple Bundesländer
- additional layouts
- reduced or removed attribution
- organisation branding
- support

Initial price hypothesis:

- EUR 49–99 per year and domain

### Verified API

Raw holiday dates remain free.

Paid API value may include:

- calculated connected free periods
- vacation-day optimisation
- multi-state overlap calculations
- source and review metadata
- data versions
- change history
- update notifications
- higher usage limits
- commercial-use terms
- support and reliability commitments

Initial price hypothesis:

- EUR 19–49 per month for a small professional plan
- EUR 99 or more per month for business use

### Organisation feeds

Possible products:

- customer-specific ICS feeds
- customer-specific JSON feeds
- multi-state packages
- update support
- white-label delivery

Initial price hypothesis:

- EUR 299 or more per year
- custom pricing for specialised requirements

Prices are hypotheses, not promises.

## 8. Advertising policy

Schulferienklar must not use:

- full-screen advertising
- repeated in-content ads
- misleading download buttons
- large sticky advertising
- forced ads before downloads
- behavioural advertising aimed at children

Limited future advertising may include:

- one clearly marked sponsor card on an adult planning page
- contextual travel partnerships
- a small page-footer sponsorship
- affiliate products on Germany Travel Checker rather than the core
  Schulferienklar calendar

## 9. Validation period

The first widget should be evaluated for approximately 90 days.

Signals supporting further investment include:

- at least five external installations
- at least three organisation enquiries
- at least two requests for professional widget features
- at least one API or custom-data enquiry

If demand is not visible, payment and account systems should not be built.

## 10. Conservative revenue expectations

Planning assumptions, before tax and expenses:

- 2027: approximately EUR 500
- 2028: approximately EUR 3,000

These figures are deliberately conservative.

The purpose of 2027 is to identify the first paying customer type.
The purpose of 2028 is to establish a small amount of repeatable B2B revenue.

## 11. Execution order

1. Update the product documentation to reflect completed work.
2. Define and build one free widget.
3. Publish a widget demo and embed-code generator.
4. Measure code copies, installations and partner enquiries.
5. Introduce the widget directly to a small number of relevant websites.
6. Build Widget Pro or Verified API only when real demand is observed.
7. Consider consumer premium products only after repeat usage is proven.

## 12. Final principle

> Schulferienklar attracts users with a free, trustworthy and low-distraction
> planning service, and earns revenue through widgets, verified data,
> calculations and organisation-specific delivery.
